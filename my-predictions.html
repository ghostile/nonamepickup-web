Your limit grows with experience. Each distinct settled game you bet on adds 10 points to the 100-point starting maximum. Wins, losses, ties and settled no-correct-bettor refunds count. Older rule-version-1 games keep their 250-point maximum. The game-limit column shows the currently applicable limit, not the limit at the time you placed a historical bet. Your available balance can constrain the amount further.

Credits are actual retained betting settlement and correction credits, including returned stakes. Cancelled and voided stakes are refunded separately. A corrected result can change correctness without reversing earlier credits.
